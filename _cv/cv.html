About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Interdisciplinary Program in Artificial Intelligence at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Gunhee Kim in the Vision and Learning Lab. I am also currently a Research Intern at LG AI Research.

My research goal is to build multimodal intelligence systems that search, read, and reason over real-world knowledge in collaboration with humans. I focus on how such systems acquire information from web sources and visually rich documents, and how multimodal large language models (MLLMs) ground their answers in that evidence. My recent research interests include:

  • Multimodal Retrieval: searching web and document sources for evidence that covers the diverse perspectives of open-ended queries
  • Visual Document Understanding & RAG: reading visually rich documents and grounding MLLMs in multimodal evidence for long-form, cited generation
  • Document Agent (ongoing): generalist agents that plan and orchestrate multiple tools over documents
